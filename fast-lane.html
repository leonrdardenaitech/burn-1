VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

How Fast Lane Works

  1. 1
    Upload what you have
    Binder, scan output, questionnaire, MFA/backup exports — whatever exists. If it's a folder, zip it.
  2. 2
    AI prefills the template
    Your uploads are mapped to the Burn-1 binder template for your framework. No blank page.
  3. 3
    System verifies completeness
    We check that every required control has evidence. You get an expected release time, not a "we'll be in touch."
  4. 4
    Human review → Anergi seal
    A human reviews the binder. Then Anergi.io signs the Cyber Seal. Nothing ships unreviewed.
  5. 5
    Receipt on your subdomain
    The sealed binder and 8-page report post to yourcompany.burn-1.com. Your carrier verifies it there.
Expected turnaround
Completeness check: minutes. Human review + seal: 1–3 business days from a complete upload. You'll see the release time after the upload is checked.
