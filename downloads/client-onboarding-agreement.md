# Client Onboarding & Master Services Agreement (MSA)
## Managed Security Assessment, Compliance Remediation & Cyber Insurance Optimization

---

## 1. Parties & Purpose

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of `[Effective Date]` by and between:
* **Service Provider**: `[Your Agency / Legal Entity Name]`, ("Provider")
* **Client**: `[Client Legal Entity Name]`, ("Client")

WHEREAS, Provider offers managed cybersecurity assessment, vulnerability remediation management, tabletop exercises, GRC documentation, and cyber insurance proof binder compilation services; and Client desires to engage Provider to deliver these services under the terms set forth herein.

---

## 2. Scope of Services & Deliverables

Provider agrees to deliver the specific services corresponding to Client's selected Service Tier (**Silver / Gold / Platinum**), including:

1. **Assessment & GRC Auditing**: Executing annual or continuous Internal Self-Assessment Questionnaires (SAQ) across 7 core security domains mapped to NIST CSF 2.0, CMMC 2.0, NYDFS Part 500, PCI DSS v4.0, and HIPAA.
2. **Vulnerability Remediation & POA&M Management**: Categorizing scan results, assigning CVSS SLAs (14-day Critical patch SLA), providing zero-cost virtual patching/compensating controls, and maintaining the Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M) log.
3. **Zero-Cost Identity & Backup Hardening**: Delivering technical setup guidance and export scripts for native M365 Entra Security Defaults, Duo, AWS S3 Object Lock, and Veeam Hardened Repositories.
4. **Tabletop Exercises (TTX)**: Facilitating scenario-based crisis simulation drills and producing executive After-Action Reports (AAR).
5. **Cyber Insurance Renewal Evidence Binder**: Assembling, verifying, and formatting dated technical evidence packages for submission to cyber insurance carriers (e.g., Chubb, Beazley, Coalition, Travelers).

---

## 4. Client Roles & Responsibilities

Client acknowledges that Provider's performance is contingent upon Client's timely cooperation, including:
* **Access Granting**: Providing read-only administrative access or configuration export logs for identity providers (Entra ID/Okta), endpoint consoles (EDR/MDR), and backup systems.
* **Remediation Approval**: Timely review and authorization of recommended patch deployments, GPO updates, or network microsegmentation rules.
* **Accuracy of Representations**: Ensuring that all internal operational statements provided during SAQs and insurance application reviews accurately reflect technical realities.

---

## 5. Confidentiality, Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

1. **Confidential Information**: Both parties agree to protect all proprietary network diagrams, security scan reports, vulnerability logs, employee data, and policy binders as strictly confidential.
2. **Data Integrity (ALCOA+)**: Provider maintains strict data governance, ensuring all audit logs and evidence exports are Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, and Accurate.
3. **Data Retention & Sanitization**: Upon termination of this Agreement, Provider shall return or cryptographically sanitize all Client assessment data in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 standards.

---

## 6. Insurance Claims Support & Regulatory Disclaimers

1. **No Insurance Underwriting Guarantee**: While Provider's Evidence Binder package is designed to satisfy underwriting requirements and maximize discount eligibility, final premium pricing, coverage terms, sublimits, and policy issuance remain at the sole discretion of the insurance carrier.
2. **Incident Notification Notice**: In the event of a security breach, Client remains strictly responsible for contacting their insurance carrier's 24/7 hotline within the policy-mandated reporting window (typically 24 hours). Provider shall support breach counsel and forensic investigators as requested.
