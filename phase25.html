// STRUCTURED DEVELOPMENTAL PHASES

BUILT FOR IMPACT: THE 4 PROGRESSION PHASES

Athletes don't hope to make plays — they create them through deliberate, progressive mastery.

PHASE 01

Biomechanics & Foundation

Eliminate wasted movement. Stance, rapid deceleration, hip sink, explosive plant-and-drive mechanics, and center-of-gravity control.

• FOOTWORK ZERO-LATENCY
PHASE 02

Urgency & High-Speed Reps

Train at game speed before game day. Execute route stems, coverage transitions, and reactionary tackling angles with suddenness and intent.

• HIGH-VELOCITY EXECUTION
PHASE 03

Football IQ & Film Study

Know what the opponent does before the ball is snapped. Coverage rotation recognition, leverage manipulation, and situational clock awareness.

• PRE-SNAP MASTERY
PHASE 04

Leadership & Life Impact

Character under pressure. Maintaining GPA standards, respecting parents, coaches, and teachers, and holding teammates accountable to excellence.
